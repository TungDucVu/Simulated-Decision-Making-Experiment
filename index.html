Consumer Decision-Making Study

An interactive investigation into modern digital decision interfaces

About This Study

You will be presented with three realistic everyday consumer scenarios: an online shopping decision, a mobile application permission request, and a digital service subscription offer.

Please review each scenario naturally, just as you would when using regular websites or applications on your computer or mobile device.

⏱️
Duration

Approximately 3–5 minutes to complete.

🔒
Confidentiality

Completely anonymous. No personal identifiable information is collected.

🎯
Realistic Simulation

Interactive interfaces modelled after popular consumer applications.

Participant Instructions

Please read these brief guidelines before proceeding.

  1. Three Independent Scenarios: You will complete three independent scenarios in sequence. Take time to read the details, pricing, permissions, and conditions provided on each screen.
  2. Make Genuine Choices: There are no right or wrong answers. Choose the option that best reflects what you would realistically decide in that situation.
  3. Confirming Your Choice: Select your preferred option by clicking on it. When satisfied, proceed by clicking the confirmation button at the bottom of the page.
  4. Final Feedback: After the three scenarios, you will answer a brief 6-question reflection survey followed by an instant summary report.
ℹ️

Please complete all steps in one uninterrupted session. Do not refresh or navigate backward during the experiment.
